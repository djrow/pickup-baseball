# ADR-0005: Privacy Consent Ledger, Roster Visibility Defaults, Telemetry Minimization, and Two-Tier Erasure

Status: accepted

## Context

Ticket #13 ([Decision: PII Consent Ledger, Roster Visibility, and Waiver Retention vs Erasure](https://github.com/djrow/pickup-baseball/issues/13)) resolves the platform's privacy and data-lifecycle specification based on [Research: Privacy Consent, Self-Service Erasure, and Electronic Liability Waiver Retention](https://github.com/djrow/pickup-baseball/issues/8), `GLOSSARY.md`, and [ADR-0001](0001-fork-configuration-boundary.md)–[ADR-0004](0004-free-tier-capacity-architecture.md):

1. **Unconsented peer & Visitor PII exposure:** `GET /api/events/:id` (`src/app/routes/events/core.js`) currently returns Event `comments` (`author_name`, `avatar_url`, `bio`) to unauthenticated **Visitors**, while signed-in **Members** see every attendee's full `name`, `bio`, `avatar_url`, `gear_bringing`, `guest_names` (**Companion** names), `waiver_status`, and (in `GET /api/baseball/upcoming`) `notes`. Meanwhile, `GET /api/users/:id/avatar` (`src/app/routes/auth.js`) serves avatars publicly without auth or consent checks, and `POST /api/events/:id/comments` permits unauthenticated comment posting.
2. **Missing consent versioning:** `users.clubhouse_opted_in` is a bare `0/1` flag with no `policy_version`, `policy_sha256`, or timestamp, and Visitor RSVPs lack explicit opt-in tracking for `Notification Preference` and `Privacy Consent`.
3. **Plaintext rate-limit & waiver telemetry:** `checkRateLimit()` (`src/app/security.js`) stores raw IP addresses and emails in `BASEBALL_DB.rate_limits.key` (and `CHAT_DB.chat_rate_limits`) with opportunistic `nowSec % 20 === 0` pruning, while `BASEBALL_DB.waivers` stores `signer_ip` and `user_agent` indefinitely and bakes them into the archived Google Drive HTML certificate (`gdrive_file_id`).
4. **No self-service export or erasure & seal-vs-erasure conflict:** `src/app/routes/auth.js` has no `GET /api/auth/export` or `DELETE /api/auth/me`, and Admin `DELETE /api/auth/users/:id` leaves PII in `attendees`, `comments`, `batting_practice_signups`, `CHAT_DB.messages`, and `waivers`. Furthermore, mutating `waivers.email`, `printed_name`, or `signature_data` in place invalidates `document_sha256` and `provenance_seal` (`buildCanonicalWaiverPayload` in `src/app/routes/waiver.js`), whereas `signer_ip`, `user_agent`, and `user_id` sit outside the seal.

## Decision

1. **Zero Visitor Peer PII & Consent-First Member Roster Visibility:**
   - **Unauthenticated Visitors:** `GET /api/events/:id` (including `?token=` self-RSVP views) and `GET /api/baseball/upcoming` return **zero** third-party PII—restricting `comments` (names, bios, avatars) alongside rosters, `bp_signups`, and task assignee names strictly to signed-in **Members** and **Organizers**. Require `requireAuth` to post Event comments (`POST /api/events/:id/comments`), and gate `GET /api/users/:id/avatar` behind `requireAuth` + owner/`Organizer`/`profile_sharing` consent checks (`Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`).
   - **Default Member Roster Visibility:** Signed-in **Members** see abbreviated `"First L."` names (matching `abbreviateMemberNames`) and RSVP/task status by default. `attendees.notes` and `waiver_status` are restricted strictly to `Organizers` and self. Full name, `gear_bringing`, `Companion` names, `bio`, and `avatar_url` remain hidden from peer Members unless explicit `Privacy Consent` is granted.

2. **Two-Toggle `Privacy Consent` & Append-Only `consent_records` Ledger:**
   - Model `Privacy Consent` with two clean toggles (distinct from `Feature Opt-In` for Clubhouse and `Notification Preference` for emails):
     - `roster_visibility`: Display full name (instead of `"First L."`) and share `gear_bringing` / `Companion` names with signed-in Members (Member profile default + unchecked-by-default checkbox on Visitor RSVPs).
     - `profile_sharing`: Share `bio` and `avatar_url` with signed-in Members across rosters, comments, BP lists, and Clubhouse.
   - Record all `Privacy Consent`, `Feature Opt-In`, and `Notification Preference` changes in an append-only `BASEBALL_DB.consent_records` table (`subject_type`, `user_id` or `subject_email_hash`, `consent_type`, `policy_version`, `policy_sha256`, `status`, `granted_at`, `withdrawn_at` — **never** logging raw IP or User-Agent), while retaining cached booleans on `users` and Visitor `attendees` (`notify_emails`, `roster_consent`, both `0` by default) to preserve zero-extra-read hot paths ([ADR-0004](0004-free-tier-capacity-architecture.md)).

3. **Rate-Limit Key Hashing & Waiver Auxiliary Telemetry Minimization:**
   - Replace plaintext IP/email strings in `rate_limits.key` and `chat_rate_limits.key` with **daily-salted HMAC-SHA256** digests (`HMAC(secret, utcDate + ':' + bucket + ':' + identifier)`), add an explicit `expires_at` timestamp (`window_start + max(windowSeconds, 3600)`), and prune expired rows deterministically via the hourly Cron Worker ([ADR-0004](0004-free-tier-capacity-architecture.md)) plus a `1%` probabilistic fallback for local/no-cron forks.
   - Retain `waivers.signer_ip` and `waivers.user_agent` in D1 strictly for a **30-day anti-fraud window** (auto-scrubbed to `NULL` after 30 days and immediately on erasure), and **omit** raw `signer_ip` and `user_agent` from the archived Google Drive HTML certificate (`gdrive_file_id`). Require an explicit `18+` attestation checkbox on `waiver.html` to bound minor tolling, and isolate `WAIVER_SIGNING_SECRET` from `SESSION_SECRET` rotation.

4. **Two-Tier Self-Service Erasure, Quarantined Waiver Hold, & Visitor OTP Erasure:**
   - Unify self-service `DELETE /api/auth/me` (gated by recent OTP re-verification), Visitor email-OTP erasure (`POST /api/auth/erasure/request` → `POST /api/auth/erasure/confirm`), and Admin `DELETE /api/auth/users/:id` (adding `banned_emails` only on `?ban=true`) onto one shared two-tier erasure pipeline:
     1. **Immediate Delete / Anonymize:** Hard-delete `users`, `sessions`, `auth_codes`, `consent_records`, future `attendees`/`duty_assignments`/`batting_practice_signups`, `CHAT_DB` keys/vault/memberships/key-shares/voice/RTC/mutes/blocks, and authored `CHAT_DB.messages` (including `original_content`, `message_reactions`, and `chat_attachments` in R2/GDrive/D1, emitting a `user_erased` `sync_events` tombstone). Scrub `reporter_id` on `chat_reports`. Anonymize past `attendees` (preserving headcount), `comments`, and `posts` (`'Deleted Member'` / `'Community Member'`). Delete user-uploaded `media_items` (and R2/GDrive files) by default, with an optional user choice on erasure to leave **approved** Gallery items anonymously attributed to `'Community Member'`.
     2. **Quarantined Waiver Retention (`GDPR Art. 17(3)(e)` / `CCPA § 1798.105(d)`):** Preserve sealed `waivers` columns (`email`, `printed_name`, `signature_data`, `document_sha256`, `provenance_seal`, `waiver_locale`, `signed_at`) intact so `integrity_verified` remains valid, set `user_id = NULL`, scrub `signer_ip`/`user_agent`, and mark `status = 'quarantined'` with `erasure_requested_at` and `retention_until = +3 years` from the later of signing or last Event attendance (with notice to the user). `quarantined` waivers return `404` on standard `GET /api/waiver/certificate/:id` requests for non-Admins and are accessible strictly via an `Admin` legal-defense endpoint (with audit logging) until auto-purged after `retention_until`.

5. **Scoped Data Portability Export (`GET /api/auth/export`):**
   - Provide a rate-limited `GET /api/auth/export` (`requireAuth`) returning a JSON bundle strictly scoped to the caller's own `BASEBALL_DB` data (profile, badges, `consent_records`, own `attendees`, `duty_assignments`, `batting_practice_signups`, own `comments`/`posts`/`media_items`, own `waivers` — excluding `calendar_token`, `events.allowed_emails`, and peer data) plus the caller's own `CHAT_DB` data (`user_crypto_keys`/vault, own `channel_members`, own `channel_key_shares` where `recipient_user_id = user.id`, and own authored `messages`/`message_reactions`/`chat_attachments`/`chat_reports` — excluding third-party messages and moderator internal notes per GDPR Art. 20(4)), paired with optional client-side WebCrypto decryption in `public/js/crypto.js` when the user's vault is unlocked.

## Considered Options

- **Redact `email`, `printed_name`, and `signature_data` in place on `waivers` during erasure:** Rejected because `buildCanonicalWaiverPayload()` binds all three into `document_sha256` and `provenance_seal`, breaking tamper-evident ESIGN/UETA verification.
- **Tombstone `[Message deleted]` placeholders in `CHAT_DB.messages` instead of hard-deleting authored messages:** Rejected to avoid retaining metadata or residual reply artifacts when a Member requests complete erasure, using a `user_erased` sync tombstone for client cache eviction instead.
- **Keep Event comments public to unauthenticated Visitors:** Rejected because comments expose `author_name`, `bio`, and `avatar_url` without authentication or consent.

## Consequences

- `BASEBALL_DB` gains `consent_records`, `attendees.notify_emails`/`roster_consent`, and `waivers.status`/`erasure_requested_at`/`retention_until`/`signer_ip_scrubbed_at` columns (consolidated into the schema migration spec in downstream Ticket #17), while `rate_limits` and `chat_rate_limits` gain `expires_at`.
- Downstream implementation tickets must update `public/js/event.js`, `public/js/profile.js`, `public/js/login.js`, `public/js/waiver.js`, `public/js/crypto.js`, and `public/js/admin.js` alongside the i18n bundles specified in [ADR-0003](0003-universal-access-i18n-and-offline-ux.md).
- Any Community fork operating in jurisdictions requiring longer or shorter tort statutes of limitation can override `WAIVER_RETENTION_YEARS` via boot config ([ADR-0001](0001-fork-configuration-boundary.md), defaulting to `3`).
