About Us

Free access to pickup baseball for teens and adults in the greater San Francisco Bay Area.

Our Mission

Pickup Baseball Foundation (PBF) provides free access to pickup baseball for teens and adults of all ages, genders, and skill levels. We foster a welcoming and inclusive environment for building community with fellow baseball fans of all backgrounds in the greater San Francisco Bay Area.

PBF is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2021 and based in Oakland, CA.

Get in touch with us at pickupbaseballfoundation@gmail.com.
