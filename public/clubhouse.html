📜 Clubhouse Behavior Covenant

By accessing, reading, messaging, sharing media, or joining voice in the Bushrod Clubhouse, you implicitly agree to uphold these community standards:

  • No Dehumanization: Zero slurs, hate speech, targeted humiliation, or treating any person as less than human based on identity, background, ability, or status.
  • No Harassment or Unwanted Contact: No intimidation, stalking, pile-ons, threats, verbal abuse, or continuing unwanted DMs after someone asks you to stop or mutes you.
  • Welcoming Goodwill: Co-ed, inclusive, all skill levels (beginners to college veterans). Hype great plays on both teams and leave mocking or aggressive trash-talk out of the dugout.
  • Respect Privacy & Never Doxx: Protect teammates' full names, contact details, locations, and private messages. Do not screenshot or share private boards, E2EE DMs, or personal info outside the Clubhouse without consent.
  • Constructive Disagreement & Field Separation: Critique ideas cleanly, never attack people. A disagreement or personal mute in chat must never carry over into hostility, exclusion, or unsafe play at Sunday pickup games.
  • No Spam, Scams, or Unsafe Links: Keep boards focused on baseball and community coordination—no phishing, malicious files, or unapproved commercial solicitation.
  • Accountable Stewardship: Report violations via 🚩 with a clear reason. Moderators may hide messages or mute persistent or severe violators from Clubhouse while keeping Sunday pickup baseball fair and accessible.
