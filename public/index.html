How Our Pickup Baseball Program Works

👥

1. RSVP Anytime

Sign up in seconds as a guest or with just your name & email. Knowing player numbers helps us balance teams and pick the right field.

🎒

2. Coordinate Gear & Duties

Sign up to bring bases, balls, catcher's gear, or first aid. Tell everyone in advance what equipment you can share.

📬

3. Email Reminders

You'll receive an instant confirmation upon RSVP, plus an automated reminder email the day before game day with field details and rosters.

💬

4. Game Discussion

Discuss pre-game weather, field conditions, carpools, or share post-game photos and recaps directly on the event board.
