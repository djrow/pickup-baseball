⚾ Sunday Afternoon Routine (12:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

Every Sunday at Bushrod Park Field 1 · All skill levels welcome
12:00 PM – 12:30 PM
Warmups & Throwing
Stretch, loosen arms with catch, and set up the bases.
12:30 PM – 1:30 PM
Batting Practice (BP)
1 full hour of machine-pitch BP while fielders shag balls.
1:30 PM – ~3:15 PM
Pickup Game
Balanced teams, continuous batting order & free defensive rotations.
3:15 PM – 4:00 PM
Fielding Drills & Care
Grounders and fly balls for extra reps, then drag and rake the infield dirt.

How Our Pickup Baseball Program Works

1. RSVP Anytime

Sign up in seconds as a guest or with just your name & email. Knowing player numbers helps us balance teams and pick the right field.

2. Coordinate Gear & Tasks

Sign up to bring bases, balls, catcher's gear, or first aid. Tell everyone in advance what equipment you can share.

3. Email Reminders

You'll receive an instant confirmation upon RSVP, plus an automated reminder email the day before game day with field details and rosters.

4. Game Discussion

Discuss pre-game weather, field conditions, carpools, or share post-game photos and recaps directly on the event board.
